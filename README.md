## 📖 What Is Ad Astra?

**Ad Astra** (Latin for "To the Stars") is a comprehensive space mod for Minecraft Java Edition. It transforms the game into an astronaut's playground, adding five celestial bodies, four tiers of rockets, a planetary rover, and a full technology tree centered around surviving in the vacuum of space.

The mod is designed around **progression** — you don't just build a rocket and fly away. You need to generate oxygen, refine fuel, build machines, and prepare for each planet's unique hazards. Every celestial body has its own materials, mobs, and challenges, creating a satisfying loop of exploration and advancement.

**Key highlights:**
- ✅ **Five celestial bodies** — Moon, Mars, Venus, Mercury, and Glacio
- ✅ **Four rocket tiers** — Progressive interplanetary travel
- ✅ **Rover vehicle** — Traverse alien terrain
- ✅ **Oxygen system** — Survive in space and on airless worlds
- ✅ **Tech machines** — Fuel refining, water pumping, cable, fluid pipes
- ✅ **250+ building blocks** — Planet-specific bricks, stones, and metal plating
- ✅ **In-game guidebook** — Explains everything you need to progress
